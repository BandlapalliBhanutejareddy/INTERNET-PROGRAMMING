About the Central Library

The Central Library serves as the academic knowledge hub for engineering and technology disciplines. It houses over 50,000 volumes, peer-reviewed journals, and digital e-resources.

Borrowing Guidelines

  1. Every undergraduate student is entitled to borrow up to 3 books simultaneously.
  2. The standard loan period is 14 days from the date of issue.
  3. Overdue books incur a penalty fine of ₹5.00 per day per volume.
